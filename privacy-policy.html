This Privacy Policy explains how Rekindled (the "App") collects, uses, stores, and shares information when you use the App and related services (collectively, the "Service"). If you do not agree with this Policy, do not use the Service.

Short summary:
  • The App is for adults 18+ only and may contain mature or spicy topics.
  • No ads and no selling of personal information.
  • We use purchase history and usage data for tracking purposes (e.g., analytics and ad measurement via Meta/Facebook SDK when you grant App Tracking Transparency permission).
  • We share data with your chosen partner (if you pair) and with essential service providers (e.g., Supabase, Apple, Superwall) to operate the Service.
  • If you grant tracking permission, we may share limited data with Meta (Facebook SDK) for analytics and ad measurement; otherwise we do not use the Facebook SDK for tracking.
  • We use encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS), and for certain features (like moments media and live location) the App may encrypt content on your device before upload.
  • Account deletion is available in-app and deletes your backend data via a server procedure.

1) Information We Collect

We collect information you provide, information generated by your use of the Service, and limited device information needed to operate the App.

1.1 Account & profile information

  • Email address (required)
  • Name/display name (required)
  • Profile attributes you choose to provide (e.g., gender/sex selection, avatar photo)
  • Subscription status flags (to unlock features)

1.2 Relationship and wellness content you create

  • Survey/onboarding answers (relationship preferences and related information you enter)
  • Daily mood entries and reflections
  • Daily prompts ("Daily Spark") responses
  • Weekly check-ins and ratings
  • Relationship "moments" (text and optional encrypted photo)
  • Saved date ideas, planning preferences, and completion status

1.3 Partner pairing and shared features

  • Pairing data (to link two accounts as a couple)
  • Shared progress / streaks / achievements (to show joint progress)

1.4 Location data (optional)

If you enable the live map feature, we process your location to share with your partner. The App requests When In Use location permission.

  • Live location (latitude/longitude)
  • Optional metadata such as speed and heading (if available)

You can disable location sharing at any time in-app and/or in iOS Settings.

1.5 Photos / camera / microphone (optional)

  • Camera access (for taking profile photos and certain in-app photo features)
  • Photo library access (for selecting photos)
  • Microphone access (for recording videos and audio that you choose to share in the app, if available)
  • Photo/video uploads (for features such as moments or posts, if available)

1.6 Calendar (optional)

If you grant calendar access, we can create date-night events. On supported iOS versions, we request write-only access when available. Depending on your iOS version and what you grant, iOS may allow event read and/or write operations.

1.7 Notifications (optional)

  • Push notification token (device token) to deliver notifications
  • Notification preferences/state (to schedule reminders)

1.8 Community content (optional)

If you use community features, we process:

  • Questions and answers you post, and associated reactions/upvotes
  • Optional anonymous posting flag (your display name may be hidden to other users)

1.9 Purchases and subscriptions

  • In-app purchase/subscription state (whether an entitlement is active)
  • StoreKit / Apple receipt and transaction metadata handled by Apple; we do not receive your payment card data

2) How We Use Information

  • Provide the Service: authentication, pairing, syncing your data, and showing features
  • Personalization: tailoring prompts, suggestions, and insights based on your inputs
  • AI features: generating advice and date ideas (see "AI Processing" below)
  • Tracking and analytics: we use purchase history data (e.g., subscription state, transaction events) and usage data (e.g., app events, feature interactions) for tracking purposes, including analytics and ad measurement via the Meta (Facebook) SDK when you have granted App Tracking Transparency (ATT) permission
  • Safety and integrity: preventing abuse, enforcing limits, and securing accounts
  • Customer support: respond to inquiries at the email above

3) How We Share Information

3.1 Sharing with your partner (you control pairing)

If you pair with a partner, certain information is shared within your couple experience by design (e.g., moods, check-ins, shared progress, and any content you choose to share).

3.2 Service providers (processors)

We use essential vendors to operate the Service. We share the minimum information needed for them to provide their services:

Provider Purpose Data categories involved
Supabase Backend hosting: authentication, database, file storage, realtime updates, and Edge Functions Account/profile data, relationship content you create, push tokens, encrypted photos, encrypted live location payloads, community posts, and app state needed to provide features
Apple (iOS / APNs / StoreKit / EventKit) Platform services: push delivery, in-app purchases, calendar permissions, system APIs Device token for push delivery; purchase/receipt processing handled by Apple; calendar/event operations as permitted by you
Superwall Subscription paywall and entitlement presentation Subscription/entitlement state and related app events needed to show the paywall and measure paywall performance (we do not provide payment card details)

Meta (Facebook SDK) Analytics and ad measurement when you have granted App Tracking Transparency permission When tracking is authorized: app events and device/advertiser identifiers for measurement and attribution. When you deny tracking or have not yet been prompted, the SDK does not collect this data.

3.3 Legal, safety, and business transfers

We may disclose information if required by law, to protect rights/safety, or as part of a merger/acquisition, subject to applicable law.

4) AI Processing

The App includes AI features (e.g., relationship insights, chat, date idea generation). AI requests are sent to our backend (Supabase Edge Functions). The backend may call third-party AI model APIs (such as OpenAI) to produce an answer.

  • What is sent: the prompt and the minimum necessary context to generate the requested output (which may include text you enter and/or relevant app context you request us to use).
  • What is not required: your payment card data (never collected by us) and your Apple ID credentials (never collected by us).

5) Security

5.1 Encryption in transit

Network traffic between the App and our backend is protected using industry-standard transport encryption (e.g., HTTPS/TLS).

5.2 Additional safeguards

  • Access controls: we use authentication and database access rules to limit access to your data.
  • Provider security: our infrastructure providers may apply encryption at rest and other security controls as part of their services.
  • Data minimization: we aim to collect and share only what is needed to provide the Service.

No security system is perfect. You should use discretion when sharing sensitive content.

6) Data Retention

  • Account data: kept while your account is active.
  • Community posts: older posts may be marked inactive (e.g., after ~30 days) as part of community hygiene, subject to feature behavior.
  • Temporary content: some photo content may expire (e.g., photo posts created to expire after ~24 hours unless saved by a partner), subject to in-app behavior.
  • Account deletion: when you delete your account in-app, we run a backend deletion procedure intended to remove your associated backend data and sign you out.
  • Legal/financial records: we may retain limited records required for legal compliance (e.g., transaction-related records) even after account deletion.

7) Your Rights and Choices

  • Access and update: you can update certain profile info in the App.
  • Permissions: you can enable/disable Photos, Camera, Location, Calendar, and Notifications via iOS Settings and in-app settings where available.
  • Delete your account: available in the App (Profile → Delete Account). This action is intended to permanently delete your account and associated data.

If you need help, contact us at support@rekindledapp.com.

8) Children's Privacy

The Service is intended for adults 18 years of age or older only. It is not directed to children or minors and may contain mature or spicy content. The minimum age to use the Service is 18. If you are under 18, you may not use the Service. If you believe a minor has provided personal information, contact us and we will take appropriate steps to delete it.

9) Cookies / Tracking

Yes, we use purchase history data and usage data for tracking purposes. Specifically, when you grant App Tracking Transparency (ATT) permission, we share purchase history data (such as subscription events and transaction metadata) and usage data (such as app events and feature interactions) with Meta (Facebook SDK) for analytics and ad measurement.

If you deny ATT or have not yet been prompted, we do not enable such collection via the Meta SDK. We do not sell personal information. We may use local device storage (e.g., caches) to improve performance.
